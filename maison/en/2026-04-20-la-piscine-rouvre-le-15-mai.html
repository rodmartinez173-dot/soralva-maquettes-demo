20 April 2026 · Hélène and François

The pool reopens on 15 May

The pool has been emptied and the coping stones repointed. The water warms up with the June sun, and the terrace has gained four sun loungers.

Every spring, the pool gets ready before we do. This year, it was emptied and cleaned, the pale coping stones were repointed one by one, and the pool was slowly refilled. The pool reopens on 15 May and stays open until September. It is not heated: the water warms up with the sun, and it is in June that it becomes really pleasant.

It is protected by a fence that meets safety standards, with a gate that you close behind you. Hidden behind the rose bushes, it is ten metres by four. Four sun loungers have joined the terrace, in the shade of the walnut tree, for reading and naps.
