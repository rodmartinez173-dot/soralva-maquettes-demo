The house

Built in 1760, lovingly restored

18th century · golden stone and oak

A manor house in the Périgord Vert, in the same family since it was built. Its restoration kept the finest of what it had: the golden stone walls, the oak roof frame of the big sitting room, the original terracotta floor tiles and the fireplace where a fire is still lit in winter. All around, a garden enclosed by low walls, old roses and the old walnut tree that gave the house its name.

Outdoors

The garden, the terrace under the walnut tree, the pool.

Private terrace · pool from May to September · walled garden

The big garden is yours alone. The stone terrace, in the shade of the old walnut tree, is where summer dinners are served; the pool, edged with pale stone, hides behind the rose bushes.

Your hosts

Hélène and François

They have lived in the east wing of the house for thirty years, know every stone and every rose bush, and give their guests complete freedom, while staying close at hand for a key, a tip or a bunch of flowers.

The area

Around Villars, at a glance.

The Périgord Vert lives up to its name: it is the greenest and quietest part of the Dordogne, with narrow valleys and villages of golden stone.

  • Brantôme

    12 km

    Its abbey set against the cliff, and the Dronne all around.

  • Bourdeilles castle

    22 km

    Two castles, one medieval, the other Renaissance, above the river.

  • The Villars caves

    3 km

    Prehistoric paintings and halls of crystals.

  • Saint-Jean-de-Côle

    9 km

    One of the most beautiful villages in France, its square and its bridge.
