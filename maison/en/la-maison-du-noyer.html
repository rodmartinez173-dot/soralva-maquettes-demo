A manor house, from the sitting room to the attic.

The house opens straight onto the terrace and the walled garden: a big sitting room with a stone fireplace, a dining room for ten, a farmhouse kitchen with its original sink and a range cooker.

Upstairs, four bedrooms: the master bedroom with its marble fireplace, a second one with its own shower room, two sharing a shower room; up under the roof, a fifth bedroom with its own. The oak floorboards, terracotta tiles and panelling are all original; the comfort is today’s, with underfloor heating on the ground floor and wifi throughout the house.

The detail

The oak door

Hung in 1760, it has kept its hand-forged nails and its lever lock. The key, as long as your hand, is the one we give you when you arrive.

Your stay

Arrival and departure

Your booking

On booking

30% down payment, once the booking is confirmed; the balance 30 days before arrival.

Cancellation

If you cancel more than 60 days before your arrival, your down payment is refunded. After that, the price of the stay remains due, unless the house is let again for the same dates. Your home insurance must cover holiday stays: please check before you come. We also recommend cancellation insurance.
