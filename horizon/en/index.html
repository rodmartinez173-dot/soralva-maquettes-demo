The setting

Through the day.

High above the Dordogne, Le Coteau looks across at the cliffs opposite. All around, walnut trees, meadows and silence; down below, the river.

  1. Waking up

    Coffee at the window while the valley rises out of the mist, then a walk to the village for fresh bread.

  2. The afternoon

    The path runs down to the river in the shade of the willows: a swim, a book, a nap by the water.

  3. The evening

    On the west-facing terrace, you watch the valley fade. The night makes no sound at all.
