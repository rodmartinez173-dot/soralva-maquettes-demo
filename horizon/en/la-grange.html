A stone barn for six, under its roof timbers, facing the valley.

La Grange is the old barn of Le Coteau, a hundred metres from the house: one big room under the oak roof timbers, and three bedrooms. The stone walls and the flagstone floor are still there; the big glass doors open onto the terrace and the valley, and a wood stove heats the room from October to April.

Two bedrooms have a 160 cm bed, the third two 90 cm beds. The shower room has a walk-in shower. Outside, the stone terrace has its table for twelve and its bread oven.

Where to find us
