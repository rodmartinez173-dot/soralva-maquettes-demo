Who is responsible for your data

Thomas Lacoste, gîte Le Coteau, Chemin du Coteau, 24480 Alles-sur-Dordogne. For any question: bonjour@lecoteau-exemple.fr.

The data collected

The website’s forms only collect what you write in them:

For a stay request: your name, your email address, your telephone number if you give it, your dates, how flexible your dates are, the number of people, the accommodation you are interested in, your special requests, your gift voucher number if you have one and your message.

For a group request: the occasion, the number of people, your dates, your name, your email address and your message.

For a gift voucher order: the voucher chosen, its amount if you choose it, your name, your email address, your telephone number if you give it, the first name and surname of the person you are giving it to, the sending date you would like and the message written on the voucher.

For your review after a stay: your text, your first name, the initial of your surname, the month of your stay, the accommodation you stayed in, your email address if you give it and your consent to publication.

No customer account, no advertising tracking, no profiling.

What it is used for

Only to handle your stay request and to reply to you. It is never sold, transferred or used for advertising.

How long we keep it

Three years after our last exchange, then it is deleted. If you stay with us, accounting records are kept for as long as the law requires.

Who receives it

Only we read your request. To reach us, it goes through three technical providers who act on our instructions, with no right to use it for themselves: Netlify (United States), which hosts the website; Mailjet, published by Sinch, whose servers are in the European Union, which delivers the email; Cloudflare (United States), which checks before sending that you are not a robot and receives your IP address for that purpose. Transfers outside the European Union rely on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission.

If you accept audience measurement, Google Analytics receives the data (see below). Soralva, which built this website and runs it, acts for us as a processor: it uses your data for nothing else and never compares it with that of any other website.

Your reviews

If you leave us a review after your stay, we only publish it with your consent, signed with your first name and the initial of your surname. You can ask us to remove it at any time.

Audience measurement cookies, and your choice

This website may measure its audience with Google Analytics 4, to know which pages are read, where visitors come from and on what device. Nothing is measured until you have accepted: on your first visit, a banner asks you, and until you have clicked Accept, the Google script is not loaded and no cookie is set. Declining is as easy as accepting, and you lose none of the website’s features.

If you accept, Google Analytics records the pages viewed and the length of the visit, the website that brought you here, the type of device and browser, an approximate location (country, region) and a randomly generated identifier, which recognises two visits by the same person without knowing who they are. Your IP address is not kept, and Google’s advertising features are switched off.

Two cookies are then set, _ga and _ga_<identifier>, for 13 months from your first visit, with no extension. Your answer to the banner is kept for 6 months in your browser’s memory, then you are asked again.

The service is provided by Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland), as a processor, on the infrastructure of Google LLC in the United States. This transfer relies on the adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 (EU - US Data Privacy Framework) and on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission. Google keeps this data for 14 months at most.

This measurement is based on your consent (Article 6.1.a of the GDPR): you can withdraw it at any time, as easily as you gave it. The button below erases your answer and the cookies, then asks you again. The Cookies link, at the bottom of every page, leads here.

Your rights

You can access your data, have it corrected or erased, object to its use or ask for it to be restricted, by writing to bonjour@lecoteau-exemple.fr. If our answer does not satisfy you, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority, cnil.fr.
