Booking

A request sent through the form, by phone or by email is not yet a booking. Your stay is booked once we have confirmed it to you in writing and your deposit has reached us.

Deposit, balance, payment and security deposit

On confirmation, a deposit of 25%; the balance 15 days before arrival. Payment by bank transfer or holiday vouchers (chèques-vacances). Security deposit of €300, by bank transfer, returned within 7 days.

Arrival, departure and capacity

Self check-in from 4 pm, departure by 10 am; we send you the key safe code the day before. Le Coteau sleeps 4 people, La Grange 6, children included.

Gîte rules

No smoking, including on the terrace. No pets, so as not to disturb the neighbouring livestock. Parties only by arrangement, for a group renting both gîtes.

Cancellation

If you cancel more than 30 days before your arrival, your deposit is refunded. After that, we keep it. Should we have to cancel, we would refund you twice the deposit.

Inventory on arrival and departure

There is no inventory check in your presence: let us know of anything wrong within two hours of your arrival, with a photo. The gîte is to be left tidy, with the washing-up done.

Insurance

Your home insurance must cover holiday stays. We also recommend cancellation insurance.

Complaints and mediation

A problem during your stay? Tell us straight away, and we will look for a solution on the spot. After your stay, write to us at bonjour@lecoteau-exemple.fr.

If our answer does not satisfy you, you can refer the matter free of charge to the consumer mediator we belong to: Médiation Tourisme et Voyage, mtv.travel.

Gift cards

The voucher can neither be refunded nor exchanged for cash. It is booked like a stay, subject to availability; once booked, the booking terms apply.
