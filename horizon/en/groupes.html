Where you will sleep

A gîte each, all on the same hillside.

  • Le Coteau2 bedrooms: 1 double bed, 2 single beds · 4 peopleSee Le Coteau
  • La Grange3 bedrooms: 2 double beds, 2 single beds · 6 peopleSee La Grange

10 people: 3 double beds and 4 single beds. Cot and high chair on request.

Shared spaces

Two terraces facing the valley.

The big terrace of La Grange and its table for twelve, the meadow under the walnut trees for games and long evenings. Under the roof timbers of La Grange, a 40 m² room for meetings.

Meals

Cook outdoors, or be cooked for.

Each gîte has its own kitchen, and the terrace of La Grange its bread oven. Thomas and Marie will put you in touch with a cook from the village and a caterer in Le Buisson.

Things to do together

The Dordogne at the foot of the hillside.

Canoeing on the Dordogne, the walnut trail that starts at the gate, the greenway by bike. We will put you in touch with the canoe hire in Le Buisson.
