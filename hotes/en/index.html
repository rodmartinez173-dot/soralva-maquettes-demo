Your hosts

We are Claire and Marc

Le Tilleul was the farm of Marc’s grandparents. We spent three years restoring it, stone by stone, and we live here all year round. Claire makes breakfast with jams from the orchard, and Marc knows every path down to the Dronne. Your room is your own home, and we are never far away.

We speak French, English and Spanish. Follow our daily life on Instagram

What we share

Mornings under the lime tree, evenings at our table.

In the morning, we all meet under the lime tree.

Bread from Bourdeilles, our plum and fig jams, honey from our neighbour, eggs from our hens and fruit from the orchard.

8 am – 10 amincluded in the room rate

“The jams are mine. The bread is from the baker: she opens at 7 am.”Claire

Three evenings a week, we dine together.

Claire cooks whatever the garden and Brantôme market have to offer that day, and Marc looks after the wine.

For example: garden soup, free-range chicken, plum tart.
