Shared spaces

The whole house, just for you.

The sitting room and its library, the big kitchen table that seats twelve, and the garden under the lime tree. There is no separate room: we gather in the sitting room, with the doors open onto the courtyard.

Meals

Claire cooks for everyone.

The table d’hôtes for the group, €32 per person, with vegetables from the garden; breakfast is included. For a wedding, we can put you in touch with two caterers in Brantôme.

Things to do together

Canoeing down the Dronne from Brantôme; the cliff walk, starting from the courtyard; a visit to Bourdeilles castle. We book for you.
