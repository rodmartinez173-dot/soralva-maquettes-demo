Little touches by the bedside

A book about the region, flowers from the garden, a bottle of cool water: what we leave beside each bed.

It isn’t much, but it matters to us. Beside each bed, we leave a book about the region: an account of a walk, a local novel, something to read for a few pages before you fall asleep. We choose them ourselves, as we go along in our own reading.

There is also a little posy picked in the garden that morning, roses in June, dahlias at the end of the summer, and a bottle of cool water. These gestures cost almost nothing, and yet they get noticed, our guests tell us. Once the linen is washed and the bed made, Claire goes into each room one last time to make sure everything is in its place.
