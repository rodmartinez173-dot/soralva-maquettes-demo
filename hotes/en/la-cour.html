The lime tree room: you can hear it rustle in the morning.

La Cour takes up the north corner of the house, on the ground floor, under exposed oak beams. The double bed is wrought iron, the bed linen washed linen; the door opens straight onto the cobbled courtyard, in the shade of the lime tree.

The shower room is private, with a walk-in shower and a wall of golden stone. The room has its own entrance from the courtyard: you come and go without walking through the house. No television, good wifi.

Where to find us

On the edge of the village, a stone’s throw from the Dronne.

Le Tilleul, 3 rue du Pré, 24310 Bourdeilles

From Périgueux, follow signs to Brantôme then Bourdeilles; rue du Pré starts from the village square, on the right after the church. Green gate, park in the courtyard.
